# a career in 3D

A WebGL timeline of Sanskriti Bhatnagar's multidisciplinary career (2012 to 2026),
built from [the story data](https://www.bsanskriti.com/story).

Drag to look around. Click a bubble to step inside it from the left x axis and
take a ground-level journey through that era; images float scattered in the space,
the timeline line stays visible. Esc to come back out.

Four era bubbles sized by metric weight (CVR, revenue, users), the skill flower
floating at the center of the foundation era, and a blurred waffle-chart floor
made of the blended career dataset.

Vibe coded with three.js / WebGL. Live: https://sanskritib.github.io/career-map-3d/
